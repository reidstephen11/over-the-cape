# Range Rover two-door (1970–84 body, early-1980s car)

Game id: `rangie`. Body: `src/render/rangieBody.ts`, with `rangieGear.ts` (roo bar, towbar, underside, rack, wheels), `rangieInterior.ts` and `rangiePaint.ts`. Tuning: `src/data/rangie.ts`.

**What to match.** An Australian-delivered early-1980s two-door Range Rover: the original two-door body (the only body until 1981, sold here alongside the four-door until 1984) with the black grille, bumpers and floating-roof pillars of the 1981–84 cars, before the 1986 facelift's plastic grille. The 3.5 L Rover V8 on carburettors, the LT77 five-speed with the LT230 transfer (full-time four-wheel drive with a centre diff lock), coil springs on both live axles, discs all round, 205 R16 tyres on 6 × 16 Rostyle steel wheels. The roo bar with its winch and two driving lamps, the towbar, the gutter rack and the second spare are added for the game; the green Australian car in the photographs carries the same kind of bar and lamps.

The two-door body did not change in shape between 1970 and 1984, so photographs of 1972–79 cars are the same panels. Leave out what the photographs say is early or aftermarket (silver bumpers and body-colour pillars on the 1970s cars, roof racks, later alloy wheels) and everything on the four-door and the 1986-on cars. `research-notes.md` has every published figure with its source and how far to trust it.

The game's car is Lincoln Green, the colour of AQ 34 UL in the two OSX photographs (the owner asked for the choice to stay in keeping with the game; the Camel's yellow, the Army's green and the 60's taupe are already taken). The tan vinyl of the cabin is in the Jeremy-from-Sydney photographs of the white car.

The heights that set the side, read off `1981_Range_Rover.jpg` (the blue car, nearly square on) with the 0.75 m tyre and the 2.54 m wheelbase as the scale, and checked against `1974_Range_Rover_(21802347350).jpg`: waist crease (`WAIST`) 1.01 m off the ground, bottom of the glass (`BELT`) 1.27 m, top of the side glass (`ROOF_EDGE`) 1.70 m, bottom of the doors 0.60 m, sill 0.54 m, bonnet 1.03 m at its lip and 1.09 m at the scuttle with the castellation rails 35 mm higher, headlamp centres 0.83 m, wheel-opening tops 0.885 m. Front door shut −0.60, door/quarter shut 0.585, quarter glass to 1.97, D-pillar to the tail (all in the game's z).

**Check these first.** The side was matched to the blue 1981 car and the 1974 car at the studio's near-orthographic side camera (`studio.view(-40, 0.4, 0.2, 0, 0.4, 0.2, 8)` at 1600×700, `?plain`); the front to the square-on blue car (`1981_Range_Rover_1.jpg`) and the white 1975 car; the rear to the 1975 and 1984 two-doors. Judged rather than measured: the front and rear overhangs (no square side photograph of a stock car on standard tyres), the rake of the windscreen (about 30°) and the rear glass (drawn upright; the real upper tailgate leans forward a few degrees), the split of the tail lamps (amber over clear over red, from the close-up), and the depth of the waist crease. The roo bar hides the lower grille panel and the bumper's middle.

## Gaps

**No photograph is committed.** The originals could not be downloaded from the container this work was done in: `upload.wikimedia.org` answered HTTP 429 to every request from its address, over several hours and with waits between tries, while the Commons pages themselves loaded. Only thumbnails of 1,920 px or less came through, and the folder README does not allow a thumbnail of a larger file to be committed. The model was built against those working copies. `manifest.json` lists every file with its original URL, so the originals can be downloaded into this folder from a machine Wikimedia does not throttle (the Perentie and 60 Series folders show the layout); the table below is written for those files.

No dashboard, engine bay or underside photograph under a free licence was found, so the cabin follows the published trim (tan vinyl buckets, a split rear bench, the square instrument binnacle of the pre-1985 dash, the transmission handbrake on the tunnel). No top view. The exact year the Australian two-door gained black bumpers and pillars is not settled; the game follows the UK 1981 car.

The LT77 ratios, the LT230 high ratio and the 3.54 axles are enthusiast figures (`research-notes.md`); only the 3.32 low range has a catalogue source. The engine output is a European catalogue figure; Australian cars of 1983–84 ran similar carburettor engines but no Australian figure was found.
