# Range Rover two-door: published facts

Target for the game: an Australian-delivered early-1980s two-door Range Rover, 3.5 L V8 on carburettors, five-speed.

Facts in my own words, with the source each came from. Confidence labels as in the 60 Series notes:

- **A**: two independent sources agree, or a source quoting a Land Rover document.
- **B**: one reasonable source (a spec database or an enthusiast reference).
- **C**: forum post or third-party catalogue. Treat as a lead, not a fact.

No Land Rover brochure or workshop manual was read directly for these notes. Several pages were read through a search engine's summary only, which is said where it applies.

## Body changes by year

- 1970: launch, two-door only. Silver bumpers, body-colour pillars with the "floating roof" vinyl on the D-pillars (WP-EN, CCR).
- 1979: "In Vogue" trim (CCR).
- 1980: suspension lowered 20 mm; height 1,781 mm from here (WP-EN).
- 1981: four-door body added; the two-door continues (WP-EN). The UK 1981 car in the photographs has black bumpers, grille surround and pillars.
- 1982: Chrysler three-speed automatic optional (WP-EN).
- 1983–84: LT77 five-speed with the LT230 transfer (WP-EN says 1983; CCR says the 1984 model year). Two-door discontinued in the UK in 1984 (WP-EN).
- 1985: new instrument pod and door cards (WP-EN); the game keeps the earlier dash.
- 1986: plastic horizontal-slat grille, fuel injection; not the game's car (WP-EN).

What the Australian two-door was fitted with in 1983–84 (bumper colour, engine state of tune) was not found in a source that could be read directly. The CarsGuide 1983 listing gives the Australian dimensions and a 3.5 L leaded-petrol engine with four-speed manual and three-speed automatic variants; the game gives it the five-speed the catalogue lists for 1983.
