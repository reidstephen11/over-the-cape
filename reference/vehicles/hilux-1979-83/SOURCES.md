# Toyota Hilux, third generation, 1979–83 4WD styleside

Game id: `hilux`.

**What to match.** The Hilux redesigned in August 1978 (codes N30 short, N40 long), with 4WD from January 1979, built until October 1983. In Australia the 4WD utes were the RN36 and RN46, both with the 2.0 L 18R petrol four. The game follows the long one: 2.8 m wheelbase, about 4.7 m long, 1.69 m wide, a single cab and the factory styleside tub, painted red, with a bull bar and a rack over the tub added for the game.

The RN36 is the short-wheelbase sister (2,585 mm). Do not shorten the model to that.

North American pictures in this folder are the same N40 body, sold there as the Pickup, often with the 22R 2.4 L rather than the Australian 18R. Use them for the body. The two RN40R pictures are Australian, and they are two-wheel drive: same cab and tub, lower stance, different front axle. Do not copy that ride height.

## Heights, measured in v2.09

No profile of this truck is in the folder (see Gaps), so the heights were read off `front-34-left.jpg`, the white 1982 4WD, which is sharp at 5,236 px wide. On the near side of that truck the body side and the outer faces of the tyres are nearly one vertical plane. On one upright line in that plane a photograph keeps the proportions between heights, so at the middle of the door the picture gives, from the ground line through the two tyre contacts: sill 831 px, waist (the foot of the door glass) 1,761 px, drip rail 2,321 px, roof crown about 2,420 px. The white truck sits higher than standard (its roof works out near 1.9 m against the published 1.765 m, on aftermarket springs and 31 inch tyres), so the body was set on the wheels by the published roof height and the proportions kept. Stations along the body were not moved: the white truck is a short bed, and the model's door and cab back agree with it once its 2.585 m wheelbase is allowed for.

So the body under the waist was 0.62 m deep and is 0.74 m; the glass and roof above it were 0.67 m and are 0.51 m; and the bonnet, which was level, falls 0.27 m from the cowl to the nose. The straight-on front view (`front-34-1980-brochure.jpg`) agrees about the nose: its bonnet edge sits directly on the grille panel, with no band of body colour above the lamps.

The windscreen pillar runs back 0.29 m over the height of the glass (34 degrees from upright). The cab leans in above the waist: the model is 1.45 m across the drip rails. That lean is an estimate (about 1.4 m) from the straight-on front view, whose camera distance is not known.

**Check these first.** The headlamps are round 7-inch lamps in square bezels on the 1979–81 trucks (`front-34-right.jpg`, `front-utility-2wd.jpg`, `side-1980-4wd.jpg`, `front-34-1980-brochure.jpg`); the rectangular lamps in `front-34-left.jpg`, `front-34-utility.jpg` and `front-34-1983.jpg` are the 1982–83 facelift; the game keeps the earlier face. The 4WD's grille is black, in four panels of slats between three uprights, with TOYOTA on a plate in the panel on the truck's left; the Australian two-wheel-drive ute in `front-utility-2wd.jpg` has a silver grille of fine bars instead. The body-to-wheel height above rests on the published roof height, not on a standard truck measured: a square-on photograph of an unmodified 4WD would settle it to a centimetre or two.

No straight rear view either: `rear-utility-2wd.jpg` is a rear three-quarter of the two-wheel-drive ute. No free factory blueprint.
