# Mercedes-Benz Unimog UL1700L (435 series), Australian Army

Game id: `unimog`.

**What to match.** The Australian Army's Mercedes-Benz Unimog cargo truck, the long-wheelbase UL1700L. On the 435 type list that is the U 1700 L/38, type 435.113, wheelbase 3,850 mm, built 1975–1993. Steel cab standing over the front wheels with its screen pillars above the front axle, its lower front cheeks faired round the wheel arches and running forward beside a short bonnet to a black grille, round headlamps low in the bumper, the air intake stack up the right screen pillar, portal axles, 12.5R20 tyres, the spare lying flat under the tray's front on the right, and a dropside tray right behind the cab under a canvas tilt. The game's tray is empty on purpose.

Do not proportion it from a U 1300 L. That truck shares the cab and has a 3,250 mm wheelbase.

**Check these first.** The cab in `front-34.jpg` and `side.jpg` is steel, and since v0.31 the model draws a painted steel roof with a round hatch (`unimogBody.ts`); the canvas tilt is over the tray. Since v1.19 the layout is measured from `side.jpg` (369 px/m, the hubs 1,420 px apart): the screen pillars over the front axle, the cab's rear wall 1.25 m behind it, the grille 0.7 m and the bumper face 1.0 m ahead of it, the headboard right behind the cab. What still disagrees: the axles are drawn like the other vehicles' axles, not as portal axles (DECISIONS v0.11), while `side.jpg` shows the portal drop; the headlamps are round behind wire guards where `front-34.jpg` shows square housings with mesh guards in the bumper; the grille carries a star where the Army truck's is plain mesh; the roof's front corners and the screen are squarer than the photographs'; the cheeks carry one row of louvres where `rear-34.jpg` shows two groups.
