# Camel Trophy dress on the 40 Series

Game id: `camel`.

This is not a separate body. It is the short-wheelbase 4WD, the FJ40 hardtop, in Camel Trophy dress. Every body angle, and the dimensions, are in [`../landcruiser-fj40/SOURCES.md`](../landcruiser-fj40/SOURCES.md). Open those photographs. Do not model a Land Rover 90, a Discovery or a Range Rover in its place.

## What the dress is

From DECISIONS, the Camel Trophy update: Land Rover Sandglow yellow on the body, hardtop, window frames and wheels; black CAMEL TROPHY lettering and a camel on both doors and the bonnet; AUSTRALIA on the rear quarters; four round lamps on a bar across the front of the roof rack; a snorkel up the left windscreen pillar. The lettering and the camel are drawn in code, the same way the number plates are, so no logo artwork is copied.

The real 1986 Camel Trophy, which started at Cooktown, ran Land Rover 90s in that colour. The game keeps the 40 Series body and borrows the dress. That choice is already made. A modelling pass should make the FJ40 more accurate, and keep the snorkel, the roof lamps and the yellow, not swap the vehicle.

## Why there are no photographs in this folder

Wikimedia Commons did not have a freely reusable set of the 1986 Land Rover 90s. The free Camel Trophy photographs that do exist are later events and the wrong vehicle, so they were not committed:

- [File:Range Rover Camel Trophy in Kraków (1).jpg](<https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Range_Rover_Camel_Trophy_in_Krak%C3%B3w_(1).jpg>) is a Range Rover, CC0.
- [File:Camel Trophy Tanzanija.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Camel_Trophy_Tanzanija.jpg) is the Tanzania event, CC BY-SA 3.0. The 1990 event ran Discoverys.

The event's history is summarised at [Camel Trophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Camel_Trophy). Images on that article are not cleared for this repository.

## What to compare

Use the FJ40 references for the body. For the dress, compare the game's yellow, the door and bonnet lettering, the four roof lamps and the left-pillar snorkel with a studio shot (`scripts/studio/?vehicle=camel`). The snorkel head is the intake, about 2 m off the ground (`src/data/camel.ts`).
