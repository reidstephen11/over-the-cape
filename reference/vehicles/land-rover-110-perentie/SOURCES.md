# Land Rover 110 Perentie, Australian Army, 4x4 soft top

Game id: `perentie`.

**What to match.** The Australian Army's Land Rover 110, built by JRA at Moorebank under Project Perentie (the contract years given on Wikipedia are 1984–92). The game is the 4x4 General Service soft top: Truck, Utility, Lightweight, MC2. It is not the 6x6. What makes a Perentie a Perentie, rather than a Solihull 110, is the Isuzu 3.9 L 4BD1 diesel, the galvanised chassis, and the spare slung under the rear floor. The game also gives it a military bull bar, canvas tilt, jerry cans and, since v0.29, a second spare on the bonnet with the pick, shovel and axe moved to the tailgate.

## How to read these pictures

- `drawing-variants.png` is the orthographic sheet. It includes the 110 GS 4x4 soft top and the Australian 6x6. Start here for the tilt and the proportions.
- `soft-top-british-110.jpg` is a British Army 110 soft top. Use it for the canvas tilt. It does not have the Perentie's wing intake.
- `front-county.jpg` and the other `*county*` files are ex-Army 110s fitted later with a civilian County wagon body. Use them for the front clip, the Isuzu intake on the wing, the wheels and the 110's stance. Do not copy the County roof, side windows or tailgate onto the GS truck.
- `front-6x6-ambulance.jpg` is a Perentie, and it is the 6x6 ambulance. Front clip only.

## Dimensions

The 110's civilian figures are the Defender infobox on [Land Rover Defender](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Land_Rover_Defender): wheelbase 110 in, length 181.1 in, width 70.5 in. What is Australian about the truck is [Land Rover Perentie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Land_Rover_Perentie). A military bull bar changes the length.

|           | Published                                        | In the current model                                                                                  |
| --------- | ------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Wheelbase | 2,794 mm (110 in)                                | 2.794 m                                                                                               |
| Length    | 4,600 mm (181.1 in), civilian, before a bull bar | about 4.32 m for the body skin (`FRONT_FACE` −2.02 to `TAIL` 2.30); the bull bar sits further forward |
| Width     | 1,791 mm (70.5 in)                               | 1.69 m (`SIDE` 0.845)                                                                                 |
| Track     | the game uses 1.486 m                            | 1.486 m                                                                                               |
| Tyres     | the game uses 7.50R16                            | radius 0.40 m                                                                                         |

**Check these first.** The soft-top silhouette is the right body for the game, and the wheelbase matches. The body is about 100 mm narrower than 1,791 mm, and the skin is shorter than the civilian 110's 4,600 mm before you add the bull bar. Compare a studio side view with `drawing-variants.png` and `side-county.jpg`. The County photos will show whether the wing intake and the lamp positions match `front-county.jpg`.
