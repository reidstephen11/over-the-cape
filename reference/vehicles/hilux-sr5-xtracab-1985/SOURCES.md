# Toyota Pickup (Hilux) SR5 Xtracab 4x4, 1985

Game id: `xtracab`. In the menu: **Hilux SR5 Xtra Cab**.

**What to match.** The fourth-generation Hilux (N50/N60/N70, August 1983 to 1988), sold in North America as the Toyota Truck or Pickup, in its 1985 SR5 Xtracab 4x4 form: the extended cab on the 112.2 in wheelbase with the 75 in bed, the fuel-injected 2.4 L 22R-E, a five-speed, and leaf springs on solid axles at both ends (1985 was the last model year of the solid front axle in North America). Left-hand drive. It is painted gloss black and fitted the way the truck in *Back to the Future* (1985) was: tube bumpers, a roll bar, six driving lamps under yellow covers, black steel wheels with chromed rims and 31 inch tyres. The game does not use the film's title, its number plate, the dealer's sign or the lamp maker's lettering.

The photographs are stock trucks of the same generation. Three are Xtracabs, and none of those has the 1984–86 face; the table at the end says per file what to copy and what to leave. Nothing from the film or from a replica is committed (the owner's instruction); those are links, under "Film fit-out".

**Do not use the listing sites for this truck's length.** iSeeCars and CarGurus both give the 1985 "SR5 4WD Extended Cab" a 121.5 in wheelbase, 193 in length and 61 in height. The brochure's table has no 121.5 in wheelbase for any 1985 model, and 61 in is the 2WD's height. Those figures were not used.

## How the side was laid out

Stations along the truck were read off two square-on views that share the Xtracab's 2,850 mm wheelbase, scaled on the wheel centres: `side-left-4wd-sr5-regular-cab.jpg` (a 4WD long bed) and the brochure's studio side view of the 2WD SR5 long bed (page 7, not committed). The brochure's table then gives the Xtracab's differences: the same wheelbase as the long bed, 53 mm shorter overall, and a bed 290 mm shorter (75.0 against 86.4 in), so the cab is about 235 mm longer than the regular cab and the tail 53 mm closer to the rear axle.

Heights were read off the street photograph above the hub centres (the brochure's 2WD sits lower): sill +0.18 m, wheel-opening tops +0.537 (front) and +0.528 (rear), the body crease +0.66, the bonnet's front edge +0.68, the base of the screen +0.90, the bottom of the door glass +0.85, the bed rail +0.87, the roof +1.34 (the published height less the stock tyre radius gives +1.346). The screen leans back about 55° from upright. In the model these are 0.12 m lower, the origin being 0.12 m above the hubs.

**The calibrated side camera.** With the dev server running, open `http://localhost:5173/scripts/studio/?vehicle=xtracab&plain` and call `studio.view(-40, 1.0, 0, 0, 0.9, 0, 8)`. At 1280×720 that view is 128.7 px/m with the front axle at x 456.6 and the hubs at y 491.3. In the 1,920 px copy of the side photograph the front wheel's centre is at (645, 972) and the rear's at (1150, 972); scale the photograph by 0.726 and put its front wheel on the render's. v1.78's outline lies on the photograph's nose, bonnet, screen, roof, door, sill and bed rail within about 20 mm; the cab back and the tail differ by the Xtracab's 235 mm and 53 mm, as they should.

**Added for the game, not on the film truck:** a winch on a plate between the frame horns with its fairlead between the bumper tubes; a tow tongue and ball under the rear bar; the two recovery boards in the bed; the plates (RN-066, after the RN66 chassis code in 1985 SR5 Xtracab 4x4 VINs). **Game liberty:** the yellow covers are drawn in the driving-lamp material, so all six light up with high beam. A real cover is opaque and comes off first.

## Not confirmed

- **Tyre size.** Greg's says 31 inch; RealTruck says 33 inch Goodyear Wrangler Radials. The restorer was followed. The 10.50 in width and the R15 are the common 31 inch size for a 15 inch wheel, not a figure from a source.
- **Lift.** Greg's says stock height. RealTruck guesses a 2 to 3 inch lift ("Our best guess"), and Silodrome writes "a suspension lift". Stock height was followed.
- **Roll bar.** RealTruck says the first scene shows a single hoop and the last a double hoop. The model has the double hoop of the RL GNZLZ replica. How the feet are fixed (to the bed floor here) could not be seen.
- **Rear bumper.** No rear view of the film truck or of a replica could be opened. Two tubes like the front are an assumption.
- **Lamp covers.** A comment on IMCDb, reported by a search and not read first-hand, says the covers appear only in Part III.
- **Front hubs.** Greg's lists "Solid Axle w/ Auto Locking Hubs"; Silodrome says "locking front hubs" and the replicas carry manual hubs with a red dial. The game gives it manual hubs.
- **Wheel width and offset**, and so the real track on these wheels.
- **Whether the two film trucks differed.** Silodrome says the first film's truck was wrecked after a raffle and a second, "almost identical", was built for the sequels. Greg's list describes the second.
- **Chassis code.** IMCDb files the truck as RN65; 1985 SR5 Xtracab 4x4 VINs in sale listings begin JT4RN66.
- **Red line.** 5,500 rpm is not in the brochure.
- **The tailgate's lettering colour.** TOYOTA is drawn pressed into the black tailgate, as the stock tailgate is; whether the film truck's letters were picked out in a colour is not known.
- **Vent windows, the SR5 badges and stripes.** The door has a vent-window divider as the replicas show. The model has no SR5 badge and no stripes (the replicas have none).

## Gaps

- **No square-on side view of an Xtracab.** The side check is made on a regular-cab long bed of the same wheelbase and on the brochure. The quarter window and the cab's back are placed from the brochure's table and the three-quarter views.
- **No straight front or straight rear view**, and no top view.
- **No committed interior of an SR5**: both interior photographs are base models without the tachometer.
- **The committed files are not the originals.** See below.
