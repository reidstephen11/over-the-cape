# Toyota Land Cruiser 79 single cab, 2024-on face, as modified in the game

Game id: `lc79`.

**What to match.** A VDJ79 single cab with the front Toyota introduced for 2024 (announced in 2023). The game's truck is not stock. DECISIONS v0.22: 4.5 L 1VD-FTV twin-turbo diesel V8, 4 inch lift, rear leaves swapped for coils, 37×12.5R17 tyres on spacers (1.6 m track), steel bar with a winch, driving lamps and a light bar, snorkel on the right pillar, alloy tray, black canopy with gullwing doors, one spare on the canopy's rear door and one on the rack. The cab is the 70 Series cab.

Read the photographs in three layers:

1. **2024 front clip.** `front-34-single-cab.jpg` is a 2024 single cab (African GRJ petrol; the face is the facelift, the badges are not the Australian diesel). `front-34-double-cab.jpg` is the same face on a double cab. The J71 Bahrain pictures are the 2024 wagon: same lamps and grille, wrong body behind the cab.
2. **The built-up 79 before the facelift.** `pre-facelift-single-cab.jpg` is a 2022 double-cab ute (v1.94: the name says single cab, but it has four doors) fitted with a winch bar, a snorkel and a light bar. Use it for those parts and the scoop. Its lamps are the old face and its cab is the wrong one. `front-34-single-cab.jpg` is the only single cab here.
3. **The modifications** are the owner's brief, not something a stock photo will show. No freely licensed photo of a 4-inch-lifted 79 on 37-inch tyres with this canopy was found, so those parts are not in this folder.

## Dimensions

Wheelbase 3,180 mm, and the family's lengths, widths and heights, are the J70 infobox on [Toyota Land Cruiser (J70)](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toyota_Land_Cruiser_(J70)>). Width 1,790–1,870 mm cites Toyota Australia's October 2025 specification table. The single cab is the 3,180 mm wheelbase. A bull bar, a long tray and a lift move the overall length and height off the stock numbers, so the stock figures are the cab and the wheelbase, not the finished truck.

|           | Published, stock                                    | In the current model                                                                                        |

| Wheelbase | 3,180 mm                                            | 3.18 m                                                                                                      |
| Length    | 5,245 mm is the longer of the two J70 lengths       | about 5.34 m from the nose skin to the tray's back, before judging the bull bar (`F` −2.36, tray back 2.98) |
| Cab width | 1,790–1,870 mm for the family                       | cab 1.79 m (`SIDE` 0.895, v1.22); 1.94 m over the front flares; tray 1.90 m                                 |

| Lift      | the game specifies 4 inches                         | `LC79_CAB_LIFT` 0.10 m                                                                                      |
| Track     | stock is narrower; the game uses 1.6 m with spacers | 1.6 m                                                                                                       |

**Check these first.** Since v1.94 the front follows the 2024 photographs (`lc79Body.ts`): flat-topped front wheel openings with radiused corners and splayed sides (top 0.6 m above the origin, 1.0 m across where the sides would meet the top, 1.32 m at the sill) under flat black flares 0.11 m wide; square shoulders on the guards (the clip's edges round over 45 mm, were 150 mm); a raised centre panel on the bonnet, 0.92 m wide and 24 mm high; a black grille surround 1.48 m wide and 0.30 m deep whose top rises 0.8 m across the middle under that panel; each round LED lamp (0.21 m lens) in a plain black pod at ±0.585 m beside a thick upright, the hexagon mesh only between the uprights, TOYOTA 0.36 m across; the amber indicators on the guards' front corners, wrapped round to the side. What still differs: the guard's front corner is square in plan where the real one is slightly rounded, and the cab's back pillar has no black vent or fuel filler yet. The wheelbase, the 37-inch tyres and the 4-inch lift follow the brief. The cab is the 1,790 mm body; the 1,870 mm figure is the wide-guard ute, which the game's black flares stand in for.

## Gaps

No side or rear of a 2024 single cab under a free licence, and no square-on profile of any 79: `front-34-single-cab.jpg` shows the single cab's side from a front quarter. The wagon's rear quarters are here and are labelled as the wagon. No top view. No free photo of the lifted tray-and-canopy build. No press-kit image was committed: the kits that turn up for this facelift do not grant redistribution in a public repository.
