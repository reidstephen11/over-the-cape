# 6×4 box trailer

Game id: `box6x4` (`boxTrailer` in `src/data/trailers.ts`, drawn by `build` in `src/render/trailerView.ts`).

**What to match.** The common Australian 6 × 4 ft (1.83 × 1.22 m) galvanised box trailer on one axle: low sides with swages pressed along them and a rolled top rail, a drop-down tailgate, mudguards outside the box with a flat top over the tyre and angled ends with a skirt down the outside, an A-frame drawbar to a ball coupling, a jockey wheel, the tail lamps on a crossmember under the box with the number plate between them, and white steel wheels with road tyres. The 1980s trailers were the same shape. The photograph here is a newer one: leave out the mesh cage and the signs on top of its box.

## Dimensions

A 6×4's box is its name; the rest is the game's choice, checked against the photograph. There is no single maker to quote.

|            | Typical                                 | In the current model                               |
| ---------- | --------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| Inside box | 1,830 × 1,220 mm (6 × 4 ft)             | 1.80 × 1.20 m, 0.42 m deep (`bed`)                 |
| Length     | about 3.0 m with the drawbar            | about 3.05 m, ball coupling to the back of the box |
| Width      | about 1.7–1.8 m over the guards         | about 1.77 m (wheels ±0.74, guards 0.29 m wide)    |
| Tyres      | 13-inch wheels                          | 0.6 m across                                       |
| Tare, ATM  | about 200–250 kg empty, 750 kg unbraked | 240 kg, 750 kg                                     |

**Check these first.** The mudguards and their angled ends, the tailgate's hinge line and latches, the tape and the lamp bar were matched to `rear-34-galvanised.jpg` in v0.49. The box's height and the drawbar's length are judged, not measured.
