# Toyota Land Cruiser 80 Series wagon — Sooty MkII-inspired

Game id: `sooty80`. Body: `src/render/sooty80Body.ts`; cab, collision boxes and snorkel: `src/data/sooty80.ts`.

**What to match.** A wide-body 80 Series wagon (1990–97) with the split tailgate (an upper liftgate with the rear glass, a lower drop gate), quad rectangular headlamps, a clear corner lamp, amber indicators under the lamps, a raked windscreen, a roof that dips to the screen header, and a tail that leans back from the tail lamps up to the roof. Every photograph of the black Japanese VX below is one car, and every measurement comes from those photos. The 1996 Australian GXL (`gxl-australian-*.jpg`) has the same body and the same split tailgate, with the later emblem grille. The game's car is the modified "Sooty MkII" build ([Repco's rundown](https://www.repco.com.au/know-how/ex31-sooty-mkii-4wd-rig-rundown)): a 3 inch lift, 35 inch tyres on a 1.67 m track, a bull bar, side rails and sliders, a snorkel up the right pillar, a roof platform with a light bar and awning, and a rear bar with a swing-away spare. None of the stock photos show that gear. `touring-*.jpg` show similar gear on other 80s.

Sources for the published figures: [Toyota's 80 Series history](https://www.toyota-global.com/company/history_of_toyota/75years/vehicle_lineage/car/id60013935/index.html) (generation and the 2,850 mm wheelbase); spec listings such as [auto-data.net, FZJ80](https://www.auto-data.net/en/toyota-land-cruiser-j80-4.5-24v-fzj80-215hp-4wd-automatic-3726) and [encycarpedia, 1995 FZJ80](https://www.encycarpedia.com/toyota/95-land-cruiser-fzj80-suv) for length, width and height, which differ between markets; [Brown Davis](https://www.browndavis.com.au/toyota-landcruiser-80-series-168-litre-45-gallon-long-range-fuel-tanks/) for the Australian 95 L main and 50 L sub tank.

**Why the model is shorter than the published length.** The side photograph scaled from the 2.85 m wheelbase gives 4.53 m between the near-side bumper corners. The tyres (0.81 m) and the roof height (1.84 m) from the same scale agree with the published sizes, so the scale is right. The published length is measured at the bumper centres, which stand ahead of the corners, and several markets had longer bumpers. The model copies the photograph, because a body stretched to 4.82 m would not fit the photo's overhangs. The bumpers on the game's car are aftermarket bars anyway.

From `front.jpg` and the GXL: the grille is about 0.85 m wide and runs from the lamp tops down past the indicators; each side has two rectangular lamps in one chrome surround, then the clear corner lamp; the amber indicators sit under the lamps, about 0.32 m wide. From `rear.jpg`, `rear-doors-open.jpg` and the GXL rear: the tail lamps are tall at the corners (amber, clear, red from the top), and the lower gate's shut line runs under the rear glass.

**Check these first.** The overlay matches to a few centimetres along the roof, the windscreen, the tail and the bumpers. The flare openings are larger than stock, on purpose, to clear the 35 inch tyres: the front flare reaches a little further forward below the bumper than the photo's, and the rear flare's tail is 4 cm longer. The cab's dashboard is simplified (a pod for the three working dials and a radio), and it does not copy `dashboard.jpg` detail for detail.

## Gaps

No top view. No straight-on photo of the late emblem grille close up: `grille-late.jpg` is a whole car. No photograph of Sooty MkII itself under a free licence, so the touring gear follows Repco's description and the touring photos, not the real build.

## Setup in the game

The draft's running gear is unchanged by the v1.09 body: a 1.67 m wheel-centre track, 0.889 m (35 inch) tyres 0.315 m wide, parked hubs 0.196 m below the origin, and the snorkel head at x 0.955, y 1.418, z −0.256, 2.0585 m above the ground. Loaded mass (2,800 kg), the tuned engine torque (500 Nm), the 4.3 axle gearing and the fuel use are game estimates, not Sooty's measured figures. The 145 L tank is the factory 95 + 50 L; episodes start with their own fill.

## Inspection

With the dev server running, open `/scripts/studio/?vehicle=sooty80`. Add `?stock` to see the factory body with its own bumpers and no touring gear, and `&plain` for a white backdrop to lay the outline over a photo. The v1.09 before and after sheet, with the side overlay and close-ups of every panel join, is in the project files under `screenshots/sooty80-v1.09/`.
