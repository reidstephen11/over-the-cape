# Toyota Land Cruiser 60 Series: published facts

Target for the game: Australian 1985-86 HJ60 standard-roof wagon, pre-facelift.

Facts in my own words, with the URL each came from. Sources disagree in places and those
disagreements are kept, not resolved. Confidence labels:

- **A**: two independent sources agree, or a source quoting a Toyota document.
- **B**: one reasonable source (a spec database or an enthusiast reference).
- **C**: forum post or third-party catalogue. Treat as a lead, not a fact.

No Toyota brochure, workshop manual or owner's manual was read directly for these notes.

The 1515/1500 tracks are listed by OULU in a separate market column. Which vehicles carry them
is not stated in what I read. **CS85 places the Australian 1985 HJ60RV at 1475/1460.**

**Overhangs: I did not find published front and rear figures.** The only supported arithmetic
is the total: 4750 - 2730 = 2020 mm, or 1945 mm on the 4675 bumper. Take the split from
square-on side photographs, using the wheelbase as the scale.

**Ground clearance: not settled.** Third-party catalogues give 225-230 mm (AD, confidence C).
One search summary mentioned 185 (190) mm from a Japanese HJ60 spec sheet, but I could not trace
that page, so it is unverified. WP-JA leaves the field blank.

**Approach and departure: 35.3 and 17.3 degrees** in one third-party catalogue, for a petrol
1988-89 car (CA, confidence C). I did not find a Toyota figure. Turning circle 13.4 m in the
same catalogues (AD, confidence C).

## Australian models and trims

- Launched in Australia in 1980, replacing the FJ55 (GOAUTO, CG).
- **Model codes.** CS85 and GOAUTO use HJ60RV, FJ62RV/G, HJ61RG. **"RV" appears as part of a
  model code here, not a trim name.** I found no source describing an Australian trim called RV.
- **Trim names differ by source.** CG: Standard, Deluxe G, Sahara (VX after 1987), GLX (1989-90).
  GOAUTO: Base, Deluxe, GX, GXL, Sahara, Sahara VX. "G Pack" is used on a 1984 Australian HJ60
  (link above). In Japan: STD, G Package, GX (1982), VX (1985) (FLEX, WP-JA).
- Standard, per CG: barn doors, vinyl seats and floors, split-rim steel wheels, AM/FM radio.
- Deluxe G, per CG: lift-up tailgate, chrome wheels and mouldings, tilt steering, cloth, carpet,
  cassette, intermittent wipers.
- Sahara, per CG: high roof, eight seats, sunroof.
- Automatic, rear heater and air conditioning were extra-cost factory options
  (https://www.just4x4s.com.au/news-and-reviews/feature-car-toyota-landcruiser-60-series/14081).

### Rear doors: check the brief's assumption

The brief says Australian cars have barn doors. **The sources say that is true of the lower
Australian trims, not all of them.** CG gives barn doors to Standard and a lift-up tailgate to
Deluxe G. A forum post says only the lowest-spec Australian cars have them (IH-GX). WP-JA says
Japan had barn doors only until Oct 1981, then added the up-and-over door. No source I read
states what an Australian GX had. Choose the trim first, then the door.

## Production dates

- Japan launch August 1980 (WP-JA, FLEX). End of Japanese production 1989-90 (WP-JA gives sales
  to 1989, WP-EN gives 1990). Venezuela continued to 1992 (WP-EN).
- Parts catalogue dates for the HJ61 headlamp: 11/1980 to 05/1990
  (https://www.allcarpartsonline.com.au/toyota-landcruiser-left-head-light-hj61-11-1980-05).
- Oct 1981: power steering in Japan, up-and-over back door in Japan (WP-JA).
- Oct-Nov 1982: 2H in Japan, 5-speed for diesels, high roof for diesels, GX (FLEX, WP-JA).
- Nov 1984: 3F replaces 2F; 5-speed on petrol; **rear bumper split with a new step** (WP-JA).
- Oct 1985: 12H-T in HJ61V; 4-speed automatic for 2H and 12H-T (FLEX, WP-JA).
- Aug 1986: 3B dropped in Japan (FLEX, WP-JA).
- Aug 1988: 3F-E, FJ62G passenger registration (FLEX, WP-JA).

**Conflict:** WP-EN dates 3F-E to 1987 and high roof to 1981. WP-JA and FLEX say Aug 1988 and 1982.
GOAUTO dates the 2H to 1984 in Australia, but CG has it from 1980 launch. CG also calls 1985 a
"facelift"; that is an engine and equipment change, not the lamp change.

## The 1987 facelift

August 1987, all models in Japan (FLEX, WP-JA):

- Two round headlamps replaced by four rectangular ones.
- Instrument panel changed; audio space became double-DIN (WP-JA).
- Wider tyres (31X10.50R15 LT) and over-fenders on Japanese VX (WP-JA, FLEX).

Late 1987 for four-lamp grilles on all 60s, per IH-AU. **Round lamps may have continued on some
base cars after 1987:** forum posts report an Australian-spec 1989 HJ61 with round lamps, and
single lamps on HJ/FJ60s to 1990 (IH-GX, market unclear). Confidence C. Doors, side windows and
body shell are not reported as changed in anything I read.

## Colours

WP-EN lists Alpine White, Brown, Desert Beige, Freeborn Red, Royal Blue, and metallics Charcoal
Gray, Cognac, Gray-Blue, Rootbeer, Sky Blue, Stardust Silver. These may be US names. WP-JA gives
Japan's launch colours as white, Freeborn Red, Traditional Beige and Feel Like Blue.
