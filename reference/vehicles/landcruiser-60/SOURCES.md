# Toyota Land Cruiser 60 Series wagon (HJ60)

Game id: `hj60`. Body: `src/render/hj60Body.ts`, with `hj60Gear.ts` (bull bar, rear bar, underside, rack, wheels), `hj60Interior.ts` and `hj60Paint.ts`. Tuning: `src/data/hj60.ts`.

**What to match.** An Australian 1985–86 HJ60 Standard: the standard-roof wagon with the 4.0 L 2H diesel six and the five-speed, before the August 1987 facelift. That means two round headlamps in square bezels on a black grille, barn doors at the back (the Deluxe G had a lift-up tailgate instead), steel split-rim wheels, vinyl seats and rubber floors. The bull bar with a winch, the rear bar with a tow ball, the gutter rack and the second spare are added for the game.

The body shell did not change between 1980 and 1990, so photographs of FJ60s, BJ60s and Japanese N-HJ60Vs are the same panels. Leave out what the photographs say is aftermarket (roof tents, lifts, big tyres, black wheels, stripes) and the four rectangular lamps of the 1987 facelift. `research-notes.md` has every published figure with its source and how far to trust it.

Since v1.12 the game's 60 is sandy taupe with the mid-1980s factory stripe kit, the owner's choice. The kit's shape and placement come from `side-right-34-60-1984-mod.jpg` (the red 1984 car); its colours on taupe are a choice, not a source. `side-right-j60-mod-roof-rack.jpg` shows the later grey two-tone kit on a white car, not used.

Sources for every figure above are in `research-notes.md`, mainly the Oulu enthusiast table (citing a 1987 Toyota owner's manual) and carsales' 1985 HJ60RV listing, checked against the English and Japanese Wikipedia.

The heights that set the side, measured on `side-left-j60-mod-roof-tent.jpg` with the wheelbase as the scale and the lift taken out: belt (`BELT`) 1.28 m off the ground, top of the side glass (`ROOF_EDGE`) 1.77 m, shoulder crease through the door handles about 1.10 m, lower crease 0.76 m along the doors and 0.81 m on the rear quarter, bottom of the doors 0.56 m. Front door shut −0.597, B-pillar 0.44, rear door shut 1.319 (all in the game's z).

**Check these first.** The side, the windows, the pillars, the arches and both creases were laid over `side-left-j60-mod-roof-tent.jpg` (camera `studio.view(-40, 0.4, 0.2, 0, 0.4, 0.2, 8)` at 1600×700; the photo's hubs are at x 747.5 and 2705 px). The front and rear are matched to three-quarter views only, because there is no square-on front or rear of a stock car under a free licence: the grille height and the split between the two barn doors (52/48, the wider door on the car's left) are judged. The bull bar hides the lower grille and the front bumper line, so those were taken from `front-34-left-customwagon-1985.jpg` and `front-fj60lg-usa-1981-87.jpg`.

## Gaps

No dashboard, engine bay, or underside photograph of a 60 under a free licence was found, so the cabin follows the published trim (vinyl buckets, a rear bench, rubber mats, the instrument hood in front of the driver) and the 40 Series layout. No top view. No straight rear of a stock barn-door car: `rear-j60-spare-carrier.jpg` is straight on but has a swing-away carrier and a second car in front of it.

The Australian axle ratio is disputed (3.70 or 4.11; `research-notes.md`); the game uses 4.111. What rear door an Australian GX carried is not settled; the game is the Standard, which had barn doors.

A second batch of photographs (an Australian Standard with split rims, a dashboard and an engine bay) was looked for and not downloaded in v1.10: this container cannot reach Wikimedia, and the copy through the owner's computer did not finish.
