# Roadmap

# The Telegraph Track (Episode 2)

The owner asked for a second level, shorter and harder than the CREB. It is built in three stages from `docs/PLAN-telegraph.md`: stage 1 (the level, playable end to end) in v1.96; stage 2 in v1.99; stage 3 (the Dulhunty gauging station side trip and thicker creek forest) in v2.00, built before the owner's play test, so polish from that test is still to come.

- ~~**Wheels that a hole can hold.**~~ Done in v2.01 for potholes: at a crawl, a tyre hanging in a hole whose floor is far below the edge ahead meets that edge as a wall, and a tyre in a pothole is walled at its sides too (`src/sim/vehicle/wheelContacts.ts`). Bertie's holes are now sheer-sided potholes and hold a car. Still open: Palm Creek's rut (0.5 m, too shallow to tell from a log) and Gunshot's Chute taken crooked (a landing, not a wall).

# The Telegraph Track North (Episode 3)

The owner asked to continue building new tracks (November 2026). The northern half of the Old Telegraph Track, from the Bamaga Road turn-off to Nolans Brook, is built in stages from `docs/PLAN-telegraph-north.md`: stage 1 (the level, playable end to end) in v2.17, built without a play test.

- **Stage 2:** side trips to Fruit Bat Falls and to Eliot and Twin Falls; a traveller drowned in Nolans Brook to winch out; a 4WD coming the other way; a dead tree down across the long drive.
- **A soft bed under water.** Every creek bed under water is one surface, so Nolans Brook's sandy middle is no softer than its shoal. A bed that sinks and drags a tyre under water, set per creek, would need the southern episode's sand-bed creeks re-driven.
- **Stage 3:** polish from play tests. v2.19 acted on Codex's first one (Canal Creek's shelf marked, low range taught before the first water, the winch chooser's arrow and bar). It did not reach the long drive's bog or Nolans Brook; v2.20 checked both from the seat in a browser (the bog shows in time and now has two rocks to winch to; Nolans Brook's line is marked with a rock and two signs). Still open, for the owner's play test: is the rise at Nolans Brook too fast or too slow; do the marks at Canal Creek and Nolans Brook give too much away.

# Make the CREB the heart of the game

The owner found Episodes 2–4 boring and easy and wanted the game to focus on Episode 1, the CREB Track. v0.64 added a scored finish (medal, score out of 1000, best score and time). Build the rest one update at a time, in this order. Wildlife was considered and turned down.

1. ~~**Remove Episodes 2–4.**~~ Done in v0.73. The trailer code stays: the Army supply run (v1.06, a Start option on the welcome screen) tows one, and test mode can hitch one.
2. ~~**Section times.**~~ Done in v0.65.
3. ~~**Ghost car.**~~ Done in v1.00 (`src/sim/ghost.ts`): the quickest finish follows you as a pale car; the pause menu shows the gap. Still open: a live gap on the driving HUD and a setting to hide it.
4. ~~**Changing track conditions.**~~ Done: each run rolls dry, wet or just-reopened from the seed; bogs, creeks, current and grip scale with it, the briefing names it, and a camp restart keeps the roll. Clay churn and its visible ruts are also implemented.
5. ~~**Engine strain.**~~ Heat done in v0.67; the bow wave in deep crossings taken too fast in v0.70.
6. ~~**Tyre pressures.**~~ Done in v0.67; the timed compressor in v0.70.
7. **Other traffic.** ~~Oncoming 4WDs on narrow sections that you pull over for~~ (done in v0.69, `src/sim/traffic.ts`). Stranded drivers to winch out for fuel or plugs done in v0.71 (`src/sim/stranded.ts`).
8. ~~**Night finish.**~~ Done in v0.66.

# Sim-audience follow-ups

For players who like 4WD challenges and realistic driving. v1.35 added the manual gearbox (controller only). Its "Manual with clutch" option (○/B as the clutch) was shelved in v1.37, because a controller button is a poor clutch. The owner has permanently ruled out steering-wheel and pedal support and manual shift buttons on phones; do not put these back on the roadmap or plan to restore the clutch option. Next, one update at a time:

1. ~~**Transfer case.**~~ Done in v1.63: part-time 2H/4H/4L, manual/automatic hubs, full-time centre lock, wind-up, HUD and touch status, and camp persistence. Starts in 4H with hubs and centre locked, retaining the existing route tuning. The first scored gate course followed in v1.68.
2. ~~**Scored gate course on the CREB.**~~ First course done in v1.68: eight numbered gates on the knoll rock climb, a live score and timer, penalties for flags, skipped gates, reverses, knocks and winching, and saved personal bests per vehicle. More courses on other climbs can follow.
3. ~~**Tyre pressure in psi and more recovery gear.**~~ Done in v1.67: 18/24/36 psi settings, timed deflation/inflation, per-wheel readings and camp persistence; double-line winching through a snatch block; automatic tree protectors; rear recovery points on stranded vehicles; a timed shovel job with local relief that wheelspin wears away.
4. ~~**Mirrors and head motion** in the driver's seat.~~ Mirrors done in v1.70, including existing interior rear-view mirrors, quality-scaled updates and an on/off setting. Subtle acceleration-driven cockpit head motion followed in v1.72, with a saved Off option.
5. **Solid-axle articulation.** First stage in v1.65: fixed-length axle geometry, shared tyre camber and moving spring/link attachments. The existing massless raycast suspension and low-range flex tuning remain; separate unsprung axle bodies and their suspension/handling retune are still open.

# Performance follow-ups

Left open by `docs/PLAN-efficiency.md` (Phases 1–7, v1.44–v1.59). Measure each first, with `?bench`, `?debug` and `window.__game.breakdown()` on the owner's phone and desktop, and ship it only if the numbers improve.

- **Tree tiles copy all 42 tree geometries each** (`src/render/vegetation.ts`); sharing them would save memory and upload time.
- **Nine footprint rays per wheel** (`src/sim/vehicle/wheelContacts.ts`); fewer may hold the same contact.
- **A traffic car's `VehicleModel` is built inside the physics step** (now in `src/core/trafficCars.ts`); building it ahead, or off the step, would remove a hitch when one appears.
- **Terrain generation in a Worker**, which is most of the 20 s between Drive and Ready on a 4-core machine.
- **The props mesh stays one mesh** (v1.49 measured at most about 2% of the triangles to gain for 5–15 more draw calls a pass); revisit only if a GPU trace says otherwise.
- **One shader program still compiles after Ready** (v1.46), made by something drawn only once driving starts; finding it needs a GPU trace. Dawn and a vehicle swap still recompile, where dusk no longer does.
- **Unit-test shards are uneven** (about 545, 600 and 225 s of test time, v1.45), because Vitest shards by a hash of the path; a sequencer balanced by duration would need timings kept between CI runs.
