# Verification — 1986 Camel Trophy Ninety review 01

This is a separate review proposal, pending user approval. Neither game checkout has been changed.

- **Build:** TypeScript and production build pass. A separate copy installs from the pinned lockfile and produces a byte-identical bundle and HTML entry point; see build-verification.json.
- **Baseline:** The original Camel body's positions, normals, colours, UVs, material IDs and damage-zone tags exactly match the source game. Only a non-rendering review-selection attribute was added to the copied baseline.
- **Mesh:** Final body has 100,328 triangles versus 60,949 for the original Camel body. Each new wheel has 5,184 triangles. The complete exported assembly has 162,011 triangles, also recorded in validation-v1.json. This model is higher detail and has not been profiled in game.
- **Geometry checks:** All attributes are checked for nonfinite values before cleanup. Primitive seam triangles with collapsed area are removed, retaining authored normals, colours, materials and selection IDs. Final body/wheel audits report no nonfinite data, zero-length normals, collapsed triangles or contrary face normals.
- **Wheel clearance:** 1,960 sampled wheel poses cover both front wheels at every whole degree from −34° to +34° and every 10 mm from parked through 130 mm compression, plus both rear wheels at all 14 compression levels. Full triangle-BVH checks report no intersections. A separate solid-volume check tests 2,836 unique wheel vertices against the body's rounded/rotated blocks with a 0.5 mm penetration tolerance and reports no hits. These sampled tests are not continuous collision certification.
- **Moving suspension:** The same 1,960 individual-wheel poses check springs, dampers and radius/trailing links against the tyres. Another 70 symmetric/individual compression configurations check moving springs and dampers against the body, excluding only their explicit seats and mounting towers. Both report zero intersections. Kicked-up frame rails, a higher rear load floor, inboard spring positions, individual damper offsets, revised radius arms and an exhaust offset resolve the clashes found in the generic layout.
- **Spare clearance:** Rear-door and bonnet spare surfaces do not intersect the remaining body surfaces. Only each spare's own mounting hardware is excluded as intended support contact.
- **Ninety layout:** Browser checks verify left-hand-drive steering, 2.360 m axle spacing, 1.486 m track, coils on the proposal and leaf springs on the untouched game baseline. The nominal 0.405 m tyre radius and 0.205 m width are local review values.
- **Visual review:** 28 views cover both sides, front/rear, roof, underside, grille with bar hidden, bonnet with spare removed, open roof, cargo and rack feet, rear door/lamps, spare clamp, LHD cabin, steering locks/full bump, intake, four roof lamps, sand ladders and cage joins. These are available individually and as a contact sheet/gallery.
- **Viewer and export:** Actual selectors, camera buttons, accessory controls, stance sliders and export button are exercised. Hiding the bar preserves headlamps; restoring hidden parts restores exact indices. Wheels and suspension follow the sliders. Complete parked exports are byte-identical even with Original selected, parts hidden and wheels fully posed; the live pose is preserved after export. GLTFLoader reloads the export, checks finite positions and matching source/export triangle counts. No browser JavaScript errors.
- **Standalone viewer:** Verified over local HTTP, with bundled local code. Direct file navigation is blocked by this environment's browser policy and remains uncertified here.
- **Preservation and packaging:** Both original repositories are clean at their original revisions. Every manifest entry from the Hilux, Patrol, LC79, Perentie, Unimog, Troopy and FJ40 studies is checked unchanged. ZIP CRC and every archived SHA-256 manifest entry are verified.

Machine-readable evidence: geometry-audit.json, all-solid-clearance.json, mesh-clearance-full.json, spare-clearance.json, suspension-clearance.json, suspension-body-clearance.json, validation-v1.json, build-verification.json and provenance.json. See README.md for historical assumptions, approximate details and the outstanding integration work.
