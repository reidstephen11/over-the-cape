# Verification — modified 80 Series review 01

This is an isolated, unapproved new model. Neither game checkout nor any completed study has been modified.

- **Build:** TypeScript and Vite production build pass. A separate clean copy installs from the pinned lockfile and produces a byte-identical viewer bundle and HTML entry point; see build-verification.json.
- **Comparison:** Both modes use the new 80 Series shell, 35-inch-style tyres, raised parked stance and right-hand-drive cabin. Body only omits the external barwork, roof equipment and rear carrier. It is not a factory-stock or game-source baseline.
- **Geometry:** The complete body has 107,450 triangles; each wheel has 6,744. The complete exported assembly has 170,901 triangles. Counts are recorded in geometry-audit.json and validation-v1.json. All attributes are checked for nonfinite values before cleanup. Collapsed primitive seam/grid triangles are removed while preserving normals, colours, material IDs and semantic selections. Final body and wheel have zero nonfinite data, zero-length normals, collapsed triangles or contrary face normals.
- **Wheel/body clearance:** 1,680 sampled wheel poses cover each front wheel at every degree from −34° through +34°, every 10 mm through 110 mm compression, plus both rear wheels at all 12 compression levels. Full triangle-BVH checks report zero intersections. A separate solid-volume check tests unique wheel vertices against all rounded/rotated body blocks with a 0.5 mm penetration tolerance and reports zero hits.
- **Moving suspension:** The same 1,680 individual-wheel poses test springs, dampers and radius/trailing links against wheel surfaces. Another 60 symmetric/individual compression configurations test moving springs and dampers against the body, excluding only their own explicit seats and shock-mounting towers. Both report zero intersections.
- **Spare:** The rear spare's surfaces clear the rest of the body; only its own carrier is excluded as intended support contact.
- **Layout:** Browser checks verify right-hand-drive steering, coils, 2.850 m axle spacing and 1.670 m track. The 35-inch nominal tyre diameter, 315 mm nominal width and 76 mm parked lift offset are isolated review values. Suspension travel and all-component articulation are approximations; sampled checks do not certify continuous collision clearance or real vehicle travel.
- **Visual inspection:** 28 views cover front/rear, both sides, roof and underside, bar joins, uncovered grille, bonnet, roof removed, platform cargo and feet, rear lamps/door, carrier removed, cabin, steering locks/full bump, stainless intake, roof lamps, four recovery boards and scrub-rail joins.
- **Viewer:** Actual comparison selectors, six camera buttons, accessory hide/restore, stance sliders and export button are exercised. Bar hiding preserves the headlamps. Restoration returns exact indices. Wheels and visible suspension follow the sliders. The live stance survives export.
- **Export:** GLTFLoader reloads the GLB and verifies finite positions and matching source/export triangle counts. A complete parked export is byte-identical when exported with Body only selected, all toggles hidden and wheels fully posed. No browser JavaScript errors.
- **Standalone:** The bundled viewer passes over local HTTP. Direct file navigation is blocked by this environment's managed-browser policy and has not been certified here.
- **Preservation and packaging:** Both game repositories remain clean at their original revisions. Every manifest entry from the Hilux, Patrol, LC79, Perentie, Unimog, Troopy, FJ40 and Camel studies is checked unchanged. ZIP CRC and all archived SHA-256 manifest entries are verified.

This is a higher-detail proposal. In-game frame time, physics, damage/dirt, sounds, water intake and light-beam integration are outside this isolated review and remain untested. Historical/reference assumptions and approximate accessory details are listed in README.md.
