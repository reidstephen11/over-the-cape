# Visual audit, November 2026 (work in progress, paused)

The owner reported, after v1.83, that the vehicles still have many visual faults, the track still redraws after the Daintree River, and the menus are poor to look at and to use, most of all on the television with a PS4 controller. This file is the list of faults found so far and the state of the audit. Nothing here is fixed yet.

## Why earlier checks missed these

- `scripts/playtest.ts` stops at the Daintree crossing by default and saves still pictures, which cannot show ground changing ahead of the car.
- `scripts/ui-audit.ts` uses a keyboard, photographs eight screens and never opens a pause page or moves the highlight.
- `scripts/studio-shots.ts` shows the car at a third of the picture's width, only for the vehicle just changed, and the reviewer never saw the reference photographs.

## New tools (dev only)

- `scripts/menu-walk.ts`: every menu at 1920×1080 with a simulated PS4 controller, a picture after each press, and a JSON record of where the highlight went.
- `scripts/studio-audit.ts`: 24 large, tightly framed pictures of each vehicle (all round, above, low, each corner, wheels, roof, axles crossed on full lock, driver's seat). `window.studio.bounds()` in the studio page gives it the vehicle's size.
- `scripts/approach-check.ts` with `scripts/approach-diff.py`: parks the car along a track and draws the same view at the quality setting's detail distances and at full detail, to show what will redraw on approach. Test hooks `terrainLod` and `trackPoints` support it. The first run was spoilt (the brake pedal held at rest selects reverse, so the car was moving); it holds the handbrake only now and has not yet been read.

## Menus on the television with a controller (walk complete)

Functional:

1. Pause → Settings: Down steps through every option of every row, 33 presses to cross 16 rows. Left and Right do the same as Up and Down and cross rows.
2. Pause → Settings: choosing any option redraws the page and puts the highlight back on the first item (`show('settings')` resets `focusIndex`).
3. Pause → Controls opens scrolled to the bottom with only Back to highlight; the top of the table cannot be reached with a controller.
4. ○ on every pause page returns to Resume, not the item the page was opened from. ○ on title Settings returns to New Journey, not Settings.
5. Pause pages are taller than a 1080p screen at the television's scale. On the main page Settings is cut off and Controls and Main menu are out of sight. After the highlight wraps round, the heading and status stay scrolled off.
6. Phone driving and Joystick side are offered on a screen with no touch, on both Settings pages.
7. Pause → Map → ○ closes the map and the pause menu together.
8. To check: after the tool wheel, the map button needed two presses.

Visual:

1. Title Settings: the "✕ Select ○ Back" hint is drawn over the card's own buttons.
2. Title Settings: each setting is 180 px tall, so three and a half fit on the screen and the page is three screens long. Pause Settings: small row labels beside large buttons, and two rows laid out differently from the rest.
3. Pause main: the compass overlaps the card's corner and the "R2 Start the engine" prompt shows through behind it.
4. Four highlight styles (title menu, title settings, pause, briefing). In a settings row the highlighted option and the chosen option look alike.
5. The journey page lists one episode to choose from; its vehicle chooser is a wrapping grid with locked vehicles mixed in; Pause → Vehicle is a long list of paragraphs.

Not a fault: on the briefing ✕ starts the drive unless the highlight has been moved to a folded section.

## Vehicles (pictures of all fifteen taken; two of fifteen reviewed)

Each review compared the 24 pictures with the reference photographs. A: anyone would notice while playing. B: on a close look. C: minor.

### FJ40, Blender body (`shortie`)

1. A. From the driver's seat no horizon or sky shows: the glass is a shallow strip and the roof lining fills the top 42% of the view.
2. B (A from the front). A full-width black slab fills the lower 40% of the windscreen opening; clear glass is about 0.28 m tall against a real screen of roughly 3:1.
3. B. Side and rear glass too shallow, waistline too high (about 0.36 m of glass against 0.45 m).
4. B. Front wings too deep and round, about 3 cm above the tyre; on full lock with the axle crossed the tyre is against the wing and the snorkel box.
5. B. The snorkel starts in a box on the wing's outer skirt over the tyre and runs 1.2 m diagonally through the air to the pillar top. A real one enters the bonnet side or cowl just ahead of the door and goes straight up the pillar.
6. B. Roof spare stands on a thin post with about 7 cm of air under the tyre.
7. B. Roof rack feet hang beside the roof and touch nothing.
8. B. Every tyre has a see-through zig-zag row of triangles round the tread.
9. B. Rear arches are a low round arc with a raised lip (real: flat-topped, radiused corners, no lip), and the sky shows through them. Each holds an inverted-V pair of rods and a separate damper.
10. B. A black strip about 1.05 m long on each rear quarter panel that the real car does not have.
11. B. The winch motor stands in front of the left headlamp; the drum is on top of the bar with the fairlead in the bar's face below it.
12. C. Jerry can mounted end-on, the rearmost point of the car.
13. C. Recovery boards about 2.4 m long, overhanging the rack at both ends.
14. C. Exhaust dead straight at hub height, through or just under the rear axle, and thin.
15. C. No quarter-light, rear side glass as two separate windows, black door hinges, no step, plain white discs inside the wheels.

### Land Rover 110 Perentie (`perentie`)

1. B. Wheel arches are large half-circles with a thick black lip; the real ones are flat-topped and angular and wrap the tyre closely.
2. B. Cab and tilt almost full body width; the real cab steps in at the waist (about 1.5 m).
3. B. The roof rack stands on the canvas; no leg reaches the body.
4. B. Jerry cans hang on the body sides (about 2.2 m across); the real truck carries them on the rear face. Tailgate about 1.5 m wide against 1.05 m.
5. B. The aerial's whip passes through the canvas roof.
6. B. The front is not a Perentie's: black fine-slatted grille (real: body colour, chunky louvres), lamps side by side (real: stacked outboard), a box bumper with three hoops (real: one tube loop).
7. B. An open slit about 5 cm wide at both rear corners.
8. B. Nose about 0.12 m too long ahead of the front axle.
9. B. No spare under the rear floor.
10. C. Wing intake on the side of the wing, windscreen nearly upright, crossmember bare grey, eight-hole wheels, no roll bar.

The reviewer also found `SOURCES.md` wrong about several photographs in `landcruiser-fj40/` and `land-rover-110-perentie/` (what each shows).

## Track after the Daintree River (not yet reproduced)

What is known from the code: terrain chunks are 64 m squares that change from 2 m to 1 m spacing when their nearest edge is within 64 m on Low (96 on Medium, 128 on High), the television build starts on Low, sharp shadows reach 40 m on Low and grass 70 m. Chunks are built one a frame, and a teleport or camp restart builds what it needs a frame at a time. Which of these the owner sees is not established; the approach check has to be read first.
