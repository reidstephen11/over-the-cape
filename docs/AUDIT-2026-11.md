# Visual audit, November 2026

The owner reported, after v1.83, that the vehicles still have many visual faults, the track still redraws after the Daintree River, and the menus are poor to look at and to use, most of all on the television with a PS4 controller. v1.84 fixed the track and the menus' controller faults; its section in `docs/DECISIONS.md` has the cause, the fix and the measurements for both. This file is what is left: the vehicles.

## Tools (dev only)

- `scripts/studio-audit.ts`: 28 large, tightly framed pictures of each vehicle (all round, above, low, each corner, each upper corner from above since v1.89, wheels, roof, axles crossed on full lock, driver's seat). Give a reviewer these and the vehicle's reference photographs.
- `scripts/menu-walk.ts`: every menu at 1920×1080 with a simulated PS4 controller, a picture after each press, and a record of where the highlight went. Run it after any menu change.
- `scripts/reach-check.ts` with `reach-diff.py`: a fixed camera over a stretch of track while the car is put down nearer and nearer; whatever changes in the picture is drawn according to how far the car is. `scripts/approach-check.ts` with `approach-diff.py` does the same for terrain detail.

## Vehicles (pictures of all fifteen taken; six of fifteen reviewed)

Each review compared the 24 pictures with the reference photographs. A: anyone would notice while playing. B: on a close look. C: minor.

### FJ40, Blender body (`shortie`)

**v1.85 fixed items 1, 2, 3, 5, 6, 7, 8, 10, 11, 12, 13 and 14, and part of 15** (the quarter-light and the single rear side window); its section in `docs/DECISIONS.md` says how. **v1.86 fixed 4** (front wings), **9** (rear arches), the bonnet's height at the cowl, and from 15 the hinges and the step. **v1.87 fixed the last of 15** (the wheels' inner faces) and gave the doors their outline. The FJ40's list is closed. The pair of rods in each rear arch noted under 9 is the shock's tower, which the game draws on every leaf-sprung vehicle; it stays.

1. A. From the driver's seat no horizon or sky shows: the glass is a shallow strip and the roof lining fills the top 42% of the view.
2. B (A from the front). A full-width black slab fills the lower 40% of the windscreen opening; clear glass is about 0.28 m tall against a real screen of roughly 3:1.
3. B. Side and rear glass too shallow, waistline too high (about 0.36 m of glass against 0.45 m).
4. B. Front wings too deep and round, about 3 cm above the tyre; on full lock with the axle crossed the tyre is against the wing and the snorkel box.
5. B. The snorkel starts in a box on the wing's outer skirt over the tyre and runs 1.2 m diagonally through the air to the pillar top. A real one enters the bonnet side or cowl just ahead of the door and goes straight up the pillar.
6. B. Roof spare stands on a thin post with about 7 cm of air under the tyre.
7. B. Roof rack feet hang beside the roof and touch nothing.
8. B. Every tyre has a see-through zig-zag row of triangles round the tread.
9. B. Rear arches are a low round arc with a raised lip (real: flat-topped, radiused corners, no lip), and the sky shows through them. Each holds an inverted-V pair of rods and a separate damper.
10. B. A black strip about 1.05 m long on each rear quarter panel that the real car does not have.
11. B. The winch motor stands in front of the left headlamp; the drum is on top of the bar with the fairlead in the bar's face below it.
12. C. Jerry can mounted end-on, the rearmost point of the car.
13. C. Recovery boards about 2.4 m long, overhanging the rack at both ends.
14. C. Exhaust dead straight at hub height, through or just under the rear axle, and thin.
15. C. No quarter-light, rear side glass as two separate windows, black door hinges, no step, plain white discs inside the wheels.

### Land Rover 110 Perentie (`perentie`)

Being fixed in three stages. **Stage 1, v1.90, fixed 3, 5, 7 and 9, and 4 except that the cans' real stowage is not confirmed** (see its notes in `docs/DECISIONS.md`). **Stage 2, v1.91, fixed 6 and 8** (the front and the nose's length). **Stage 3a, v1.92, fixed 1** (the wheel arches) **and from 10 the roll bar and the crossmember's colour.** **Stage 3b, v1.93, fixed 2** (the cab's width) **and from 10 the wing intake and the windscreen's lean.** Still open from 10: the wheels (eight square holes; the real ones are plain discs with small round holes). The photographs named `*county*` turned out to be Army GS soft tops, and they confirm the jerry cans on the rear corners.

1. B. Wheel arches are large half-circles with a thick black lip; the real ones are flat-topped and angular and wrap the tyre closely.
2. B. Cab and tilt almost full body width; the real cab steps in at the waist (about 1.5 m).
3. B. The roof rack stands on the canvas; no leg reaches the body.
4. B. Jerry cans hang on the body sides (about 2.2 m across); the real truck carries them on the rear face. Tailgate about 1.5 m wide against 1.05 m.
5. B. The aerial's whip passes through the canvas roof.
6. B. The front is not a Perentie's: black fine-slatted grille (real: body colour, chunky louvres), lamps side by side (real: stacked outboard), a box bumper with three hoops (real: one tube loop).
7. B. An open slit about 5 cm wide at both rear corners.
8. B. Nose about 0.12 m too long ahead of the front axle.
9. B. No spare under the rear floor.
10. C. Wing intake on the side of the wing, windscreen nearly upright, crossmember bare grey, eight-hole wheels, no roll bar.

**v2.04 closed the list:** the wheels are plain dished discs, and a second comparison added a stowage locker behind each rear wheel and rear mud flaps.

The reviewer also found `SOURCES.md` wrong about several photographs in `landcruiser-fj40/` and `land-rover-110-perentie/` (what each shows).

### LandCruiser 79 (`lc79`)

Compared in v1.94 with the photographs in `reference/vehicles/landcruiser-79-2024/` (the 28 audit pictures beside `front-34-single-cab.jpg`, `side-wagon.jpg`, `front-34-wagon.jpg` and `pre-facelift-single-cab.jpg`). **v1.94 fixed 1 to 6.**

1. B. Front wheel openings are half circles under a round flare; the real ones are flat-topped with radiused corners and splayed sides under a wide flat flare. Body colour showed inside the opening.
2. B. The front clip's shoulders and corners are rolled over 0.15 m, which reads as a soft, rounded nose; a 70 Series' guards are square-shouldered.
3. B. The grille is a plain black rectangle with mesh up to the lamps. The 2024 surround rises in the middle under the bonnet's raised centre panel, each lamp sits in a plain pod beside a thick upright, and TOYOTA is about 0.36 m across (the model's was 0.56 m).
4. B. The indicators are on the face only; the real ones wrap round the guards' front corners, and there is a small repeater on each guard.
5. B. The winch drum stands on the bar in front of the grille with its rope drawn as a stack of rings; the plate sat behind the fairlead.
6. C. No black vent on the cab's back pillar behind the door and no fuel filler (`front-34-single-cab.jpg`).

### GQ Patrol (`patrol`)

Compared in v2.04 with the photographs in `reference/vehicles/nissan-gq-patrol/` (the 28 audit pictures beside `rear-34-1988.jpg`, `rear-34-left-1994.jpg`, `side-right-1989.jpg`, `front-34-1988.jpg`, `front-34-left-1989.jpg` and `side-1992.jpg`). **v2.04 fixed 1 to 6 and v2.05 fixed 7 to 9, which closes the list.**

1. B. The rear doors are the mirror image of the real ones: the wide door and the spare are on the left and the plate on the right. Every photograph has the narrow door on the left with the handle and the plate, and the wide door on the right with the spare.
2. B. The door spare sits low, its foot level with the top of the rear bar; the real one stands over the foot of the right-hand window.
3. B. The winch's drum stands on the bar in front of the grille, drawn as a stack of rings, and the two driving lamps cover the NISSAN letters.
4. B. The snorkel leaves the guard low down and runs to the roof as one diagonal; a real one lies along the guard under the bonnet's edge and then climbs the pillar.
5. C. No black air vent on the D pillar at the rear side window's lower back corner.
6. C. No mud flaps behind the rear wheels.
7. C. The two colours meet at the body-side moulding, low on the doors; on every photographed two-colour car they meet just under the door handles.
8. C. The body sides have no crease at handle height, and there is no PATROL 4x4 badge on the rear door or the guards.
9. C. The snorkel is on the left guard. The photographs are stock cars and show none, so they do not say which side.

### LandCruiser 75 Troop Carrier (`troopy`)

Compared in v2.08 with the photographs in `reference/vehicles/landcruiser-hzj75-troopy/` (the 28 audit pictures beside `side.jpg`, `front-34.jpg`, `front-34-b.jpg` and `rear-34-ambulance.jpg`). Heights were measured on `side.jpg` from the line through its hubs. **v2.08 fixed 1 to 8.** 9 to 13 are open.

1. B. The windscreen is nearly upright (12 degrees) and reaches the full height of the roof. The real one leans back 37 degrees to a header 0.23 m below the roof.
2. B. The roof is one flat sheet from the windscreen back. The real roof stands up behind the header and slopes up to its full height over the driver's head.
3. B. The bonnet is too high and too flat: the cowl 5 cm and the nose 13 cm above the real ones.
4. B. The three side windows are one height in a row, which reads as a Land Rover station wagon. The real door glass is lower and larger; the two windows behind it sit 0.17 m higher and have round corners. The door is 8 cm too narrow and 10 cm too far forward.
5. B. The nose is 9 cm too long ahead of the front axle (already noted in `SOURCES.md`).
6. B. The cab inside is drawn for the tall upright cab: the dash stands above the cowl and the seats are 12 cm too high.
7. B. The winch's drum stands on the bar in front of the grille, drawn as a stack of rings.
8. C. The roof rack runs forward over the cab; with the real roof it can only stand on the raised part.
9. B. The grille has vertical bars and TOYOTA in letters. The photographed cars have three horizontal bars and the lamps in the same dark panel.
10. B. The wheel openings have black lips; the real ones are pressed into the body and painted with it. Their tops are 4 to 7 cm too high.
11. C. No black vent on the rear quarter under the waist, no stripes or LAND CRUISER lettering along the crease, and the fuel filler is a round cap behind each rear wheel where `front-34-b.jpg` has a square flap ahead of the right rear wheel and a second behind it.
12. C. The tail lamps are small and low; `side.jpg` has a tall lamp on each rear corner at mid height. No photograph shows which rear door carries the spare.
13. C. No mud flaps and no side steps.

### Hilux single cab (`hilux`)

Compared in v2.09 with the photographs in `reference/vehicles/hilux-1979-83/` (the 28 audit pictures beside `front-34-left.jpg`, `front-34-right.jpg`, `rear-34-right.jpg`, `side-1980-4wd.jpg`, `front-34-1980-brochure.jpg`, `front-utility-2wd.jpg` and `rear-utility-2wd.jpg`). Heights were measured on `front-34-left.jpg` in the plane of the body side; the table is in the vehicle's `SOURCES.md`. **v2.09 fixed 1 to 15, which closes the list.** v2.10 corrected item 2: v2.09 had made the bonnet a straight ramp falling 0.27 m to a nose 6 cm too low, and the owner saw a wedge.

1. A. The waist is 14 cm too low and the cab above it 16 cm too tall: 0.62 m of body under 0.67 m of glass and roof, where the real truck has 0.74 m under 0.5 m. It reads as a tall glass box on a shallow body.
2. B. The bonnet is level from the cowl to the nose. The real one leaves the cowl nearly level and rolls down about 0.21 m to the nose, whose top edge sits 4 cm above the lamp bezels; the model has a wider band of body colour above the lamps.
3. B. The body sides are 1.67 m apart and plain. The real body is 1.61 m wide and the 4WD's flares round the wheel openings stand out to 1.69 m. The openings are 12 cm too long.
4. B. The grille is chrome bars with TOYOTA in the middle. The 4WD's is black, four panels of slats, with TOYOTA on a plate in the panel on the truck's left.
5. B. The winch's drum stands on the bar in front of the grille, drawn as a stack of rings.
6. B. The tub's rail is 14 cm too low (it is level with the waist), and the tailgate is plain. The real tailgate has TOYOTA across it.
7. B. The bull bar is 1.96 m wide, 0.29 m wider than the body.
8. C. The tail lamps are red over amber over clear and low; the real ones are tall, amber over clear over red.
9. C. Round chrome mirrors on long stalks; the Australian trucks have a black rectangular mirror on a short arm.
10. C. No black vent on the cab's side behind each door.
11. C. A round chrome fuel cap on the left of the tub; the real filler is behind a square flap on the right, ahead of the rear wheel.
12. C. One pressed line along the side at 0.45 m; the real body has one under the door handles, which falls along the guard to the nose, and one low on the doors.
13. C. No 4WD badge on the guards, no rope hooks under the tub's rail, no mud flaps.
14. C. The windscreen pillar's foot stands 7 cm ahead of the door's front edge.
15. B. The chassis rails run straight to the tail, so the spare under the tub hangs 0.19 m off the ground, below the axle, and the tailpipe runs through the right-hand rail (found by the render reviewer, not from the photographs).

`SOURCES.md` was wrong about five photographs (`side-1980-4wd.jpg` is a front three-quarter, `front-34-1980-brochure.jpg` is straight on, the two `*-utility-2wd.jpg` are three-quarter views, and `front-34-right.jpg` has round lamps). It now says what each shows.
