# Visual audit, November 2026

The owner reported, after v1.83, that the vehicles still have many visual faults, the track still redraws after the Daintree River, and the menus are poor to look at and to use, most of all on the television with a PS4 controller. v1.84 fixed the track and the menus' controller faults; its section in `docs/DECISIONS.md` has the cause, the fix and the measurements for both. This file is what is left: the vehicles.

## Tools (dev only)

- `scripts/studio-audit.ts`: 24 large, tightly framed pictures of each vehicle (all round, above, low, each corner, wheels, roof, axles crossed on full lock, driver's seat). Give a reviewer these and the vehicle's reference photographs.
- `scripts/menu-walk.ts`: every menu at 1920×1080 with a simulated PS4 controller, a picture after each press, and a record of where the highlight went. Run it after any menu change.
- `scripts/reach-check.ts` with `reach-diff.py`: a fixed camera over a stretch of track while the car is put down nearer and nearer; whatever changes in the picture is drawn according to how far the car is. `scripts/approach-check.ts` with `approach-diff.py` does the same for terrain detail.

## Vehicles (pictures of all fifteen taken; two of fifteen reviewed)

Each review compared the 24 pictures with the reference photographs. A: anyone would notice while playing. B: on a close look. C: minor.

### FJ40, Blender body (`shortie`)

1. A. From the driver's seat no horizon or sky shows: the glass is a shallow strip and the roof lining fills the top 42% of the view.
2. B (A from the front). A full-width black slab fills the lower 40% of the windscreen opening; clear glass is about 0.28 m tall against a real screen of roughly 3:1.
3. B. Side and rear glass too shallow, waistline too high (about 0.36 m of glass against 0.45 m).
4. B. Front wings too deep and round, about 3 cm above the tyre; on full lock with the axle crossed the tyre is against the wing and the snorkel box.
5. B. The snorkel starts in a box on the wing's outer skirt over the tyre and runs 1.2 m diagonally through the air to the pillar top. A real one enters the bonnet side or cowl just ahead of the door and goes straight up the pillar.
6. B. Roof spare stands on a thin post with about 7 cm of air under the tyre.
7. B. Roof rack feet hang beside the roof and touch nothing.
8. B. Every tyre has a see-through zig-zag row of triangles round the tread.
9. B. Rear arches are a low round arc with a raised lip (real: flat-topped, radiused corners, no lip), and the sky shows through them. Each holds an inverted-V pair of rods and a separate damper.
10. B. A black strip about 1.05 m long on each rear quarter panel that the real car does not have.
11. B. The winch motor stands in front of the left headlamp; the drum is on top of the bar with the fairlead in the bar's face below it.
12. C. Jerry can mounted end-on, the rearmost point of the car.
13. C. Recovery boards about 2.4 m long, overhanging the rack at both ends.
14. C. Exhaust dead straight at hub height, through or just under the rear axle, and thin.
15. C. No quarter-light, rear side glass as two separate windows, black door hinges, no step, plain white discs inside the wheels.

### Land Rover 110 Perentie (`perentie`)

1. B. Wheel arches are large half-circles with a thick black lip; the real ones are flat-topped and angular and wrap the tyre closely.
2. B. Cab and tilt almost full body width; the real cab steps in at the waist (about 1.5 m).
3. B. The roof rack stands on the canvas; no leg reaches the body.
4. B. Jerry cans hang on the body sides (about 2.2 m across); the real truck carries them on the rear face. Tailgate about 1.5 m wide against 1.05 m.
5. B. The aerial's whip passes through the canvas roof.
6. B. The front is not a Perentie's: black fine-slatted grille (real: body colour, chunky louvres), lamps side by side (real: stacked outboard), a box bumper with three hoops (real: one tube loop).
7. B. An open slit about 5 cm wide at both rear corners.
8. B. Nose about 0.12 m too long ahead of the front axle.
9. B. No spare under the rear floor.
10. C. Wing intake on the side of the wing, windscreen nearly upright, crossmember bare grey, eight-hole wheels, no roll bar.

The reviewer also found `SOURCES.md` wrong about several photographs in `landcruiser-fj40/` and `land-rover-110-perentie/` (what each shows).
